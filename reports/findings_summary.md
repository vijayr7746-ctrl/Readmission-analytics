**Risk tier validation.** Patients were split into Low/Medium/High risk
tiers using percentile-based cutoffs on the model's predicted probability
(top 15% = High, next 25% = Medium, bottom 60% = Low). The actual 30-day
readmission rate within each tier:

| Risk tier | Readmission rate | Patients |
|---|---|---|
| High | **26.6%** | 14,902 |
| Medium | 13.9% | 24,835 |
| Low | 6.5% | 59,606 |

The High tier readmits at **2.3x the overall baseline rate (11.4%)**,
concentrated in just 15% of the population — strong evidence the model's
ranking carries real operational value for prioritizing a care team's
limited outreach capacity.
